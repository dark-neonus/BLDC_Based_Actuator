Current sensing chain: shunt → amplifier → ADC

Pick a shunt, amplifier gain and ADC. The plot shows a sinusoidal phase current, what the ADC actually reads (with quantisation, noise and offset), and where it clips. The readouts show heat, range and resolution - the trade-offs from module 05.

ADC: 3.3 V range, bias at 1.65 V (bidirectional). Noise ≈ 2 mV RMS is a realistic ESP32-S3 internal-ADC figure; a good external ADC is ~0.2 mV. Try: 0.5 mΩ + gain 20 vs 2 mΩ + gain 20 - look at resolution and shunt power.
