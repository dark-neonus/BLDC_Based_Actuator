Cascaded position → velocity → torque control

Position P-loop feeds a velocity PI-loop, which commands torque (the FOC current loop, modelled as a fast first-order lag with a torque limit). The joint gets a position step at t = 0.1 s and a load torque kick at t = 0.6 s. Velocity is computed from a quantised, noisy encoder - see how encoder quality limits how stiff you can tune.

Torque limit 2 N·m (motor side), loop rate 10 kHz. Try: encoder 10 bit → torque command becomes a noisy mess unless you lower the filter cutoff, which then forces lower gains. Try: Ki = 0 → the load kick leaves a permanent position error.
