MOSFET switching: gate charge, Miller plateau, losses, ringing

A simplified (piecewise) model of one MOSFET in a half-bridge switching an inductive load current. Top: turn-on. Bottom: turn-off with the stray loop inductance making an overshoot. Shaded areas = where voltage and current overlap = switching loss.

Fixed: Vth = 3 V, plateau = 4.5 V, internal gate R = 1 Ω, Coss = 1.5 nF, loop damping ≈ 0.4 Ω. Try: Rg 1 Ω → fast, low loss, but big overshoot. Rg 30 Ω → slow, hot, quiet. Then increase the loop inductance and see why layout beats any gate resistor.
