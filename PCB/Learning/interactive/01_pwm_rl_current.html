PWM into a motor winding (R + L + back-EMF)

A half-bridge connects one end of the winding to either +Vbus or GND. The winding's inductance refuses sudden current changes, so the square voltage becomes a current with a small triangular ripple. Move the sliders and watch the ripple.

Try: drop frequency to 2 kHz (huge ripple, audible whine in a real motor). Try: small L (10 µH) - ripple explodes; that's why low-inductance motors need high PWM frequency. Try: duty so that D·Vbus = back-EMF - average current ≈ 0. Below that, current goes negative: the motor is generating (regen).
