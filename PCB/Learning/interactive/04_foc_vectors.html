FOC: three currents → one rotating vector → two DC numbers

Left: the stator's three phase axes (a, b, c), each phase current as an arrow, and their sum (the current vector, white/black). The rotor magnet direction is the d-axis; torque comes from the current component perpendicular to it (q). Right: the same thing over time - the AC phase currents and the Id/Iq that the controller "sees" after Clarke + Park.

Try: encoder error 30° → the controller thinks it puts all current into q, but real torque drops (cos 30° = 87 %) and real Id appears. At 90° → zero torque, all heat. With 21 pole pairs, 30° electrical is only 1.4° mechanical! Try six-step → torque (Iq) ripples every 60°.
