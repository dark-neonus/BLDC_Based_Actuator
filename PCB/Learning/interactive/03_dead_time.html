Half-bridge timing: dead time, shoot-through, body diode

The MCU commands the high-side (HS) and low-side (LS) FETs. Real FETs need time to turn off (turn-off delay). If the other FET turns on before that - both conduct - shoot-through. Too much dead time - the motor current flows through a body diode, wasting power and distorting the voltage.

Set dead time below the turn-off delay → red shoot-through zones appear. Flip the current sign → watch which body diode conducts and how the switch-node voltage during dead time changes (that's the "dead-time distortion" FOC firmware compensates). Shown: a 4 µs window (one short PWM pulse).
