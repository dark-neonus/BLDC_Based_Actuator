Regenerative braking pumps up the bus voltage

The motor spins a load, then brakes to zero in a set time. The kinetic energy (½·J·ω²) comes back into the DC bus. Where can it go? Choose the supply/protection and watch the bus voltage versus the 60 V MOSFET rating.

Assumes 80 % of the kinetic energy reaches the bus (rest lost in windings/FETs) and 0.1 A logic load. Note how a bigger capacitor only helps a little - energy grows with V², so you need a lot of µF. TVS energy above ~1 J (SMCJ class) for long pulses = a dead TVS.
